
** SpaceShooter **

A terminal game that depends on spaceship's movement and firing to avoid moving aliens in a 9x9 grid based field.

* Mechanics *

arrow keys to move right and left
space button to shoot

* Enemies *

Certain amount based off of levels(like 2x2 for level 2) that are moving right to left, when hitting the walls they move 1 block closer to player.

* Survival *

For now everything has 1 hp. Do or die.
